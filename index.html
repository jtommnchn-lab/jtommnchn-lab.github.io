Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check broadband availability in Nanganallur?
Send your street, apartment name, or Google Maps pin on WhatsApp to +91 9447072230. Our team checks fiber feasibility for your exact address before confirming installation.
How long does installation take?
Once fiber feasibility is confirmed, our engineers usually complete installation within 1 to 2 days, depending on building access.
Are OTT apps really free with the plan?
Yes! Select plans come with free OTT tokens. You can redeem these for apps like Jio Hotstar, Zee5, SonyLIV, and more at no extra cost.
What is the cheapest plan available?
The Fibre Entry plan starts at just ₹999 for 3 months (Quarterly). It offers 25 Mbps speed and unlimited data, making it perfect for budget-conscious users.
